# exp-031 — why-carrot-fails

**Not an optimisation.** 019 measured Carrot satisfied 1 time in 8 while Bush and
Tree never failed, and the cause has been *assumed* to be `till()` refusing ground
a plant stands on. This traces a carrot planting through all three stages —
ground/entity/ripeness before the harvest, tile state after it, and whether the
plant landed — on the real 32-drone farm.
