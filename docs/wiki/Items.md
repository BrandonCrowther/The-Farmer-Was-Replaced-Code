This page lists every item currently in the game, with the description shown in-game. Data taken directly from the game's current string files.

## <a href="Items#Bone" class="wikilink" title="Bone"><code>Bone</code></a>

The bones of an ancient creature. Obtained by harvesting the <a href="Dinosaurs" class="wikilink" title="dinosaur">dinosaur</a> tail.

## <a href="Cactus" class="wikilink" title="Cactus"><code>Cactus</code></a>

Obtained by harvesting sorted cacti.

## `Carrot`

Obtained by harvesting carrots.

## `Fertilizer`

Call `use_item(Items.Fertilizer)` to instantly remove 2s from the plant's remaining grow time.

## `Gold`

Found in treasure chests in mazes.

## `Hay`

Obtained by cutting grass.

## `Power`

Obtained by harvesting sunflowers. The drone automatically uses this to move twice as fast.

## `Pumpkin`

Obtained by harvesting pumpkins.

## `Water`

Used to water the ground by calling `use_item(Items.Water)`.

## `Weird Substance`

Call `use_item(Items.Weird_Substance)` on a bush to grow a maze, or on other plants to toggle their infection status. Obtained from infected plants. Fertilizing plants infects them.

## `Wood`

Obtained from bushes and trees.

## Retired items

The following items have been removed from the game entirely (their trade-based economy was replaced by the current unlock/cost system), but their old string entries are still present for legacy save compatibility:

- `Piggy`
- `Cactus_Seed`
