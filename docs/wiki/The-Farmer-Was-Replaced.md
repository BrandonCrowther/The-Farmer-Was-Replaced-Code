***The Farmer Was Replaced*** is a programming idler game developed and published by Timon Herzog. The game was released on Steam for Microsoft Windows under early access on 10 February 2023, before having its 1.0 release occur on 10 October 2025.

## Premise

"*Program and optimize a drone to automate a farm and watch it do the work for you. Collect resources to unlock better technology and become the most efficient farmer in the world. Improve your problem solving and coding skills.*" **~ Official Steam description**
